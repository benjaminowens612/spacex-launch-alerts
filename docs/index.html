Subscribe to a launch calendar on your iPhone. Every launch with a confirmed time alerts you 1 hour and 10 minutes before liftoff. If a launch scrubs or slips, the calendar event moves with it.

01 — Choose a calendar

    02 — Check these settings once

    1. Keep alerts

      When you subscribe, iOS shows a Remove Alerts switch. Turn it off, or the calendar arrives without alerts.

      To change it later: Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Subscribed Calendars

    2. Refresh often

      Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Fetch New Data. Set Fetch to Every 15 Minutes so late scrubs reach your phone.

    3. Allow notifications

      Settings › Notifications › Calendar. Turn on Allow Notifications and Time Sensitive.

    03 — Add to Home Screen

    In Safari, tap Share › Add to Home Screen. The app opens full-screen with live countdowns and webcast links.
