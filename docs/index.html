      Alerts in your iPhone Calendar

      Subscribe to a launch calendar. Each launch with a set time gets two alerts, at 1 hour and 10 minutes before liftoff. Scrubs and delays move the event automatically.

      1 · Pick a calendar

        2 · Check these settings (once)

        1. Keep the alerts. When you subscribe, iOS shows a Remove Alerts switch. Turn it off, or the calendar arrives without any alerts.
          To change it later: Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Subscribed Calendars.
        2. Refresh often. Settings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts › Fetch New Data: set Fetch to Every 15 Minutes so late scrubs reach your phone.
        3. Allow notifications. Settings › Notifications › Calendar: turn on Allow Notifications and Time Sensitive.

        3 · Put this app on your Home Screen

        In Safari, tap Share › Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen with live countdowns and links to the webcasts.
